## Overview

The Condensed Matter Physics group investigates the properties of solid and liquid matter, focusing on understanding structure-property relationships at multiple scales.

## Research Focus

- **Solid State Physics** — crystal structures, electronic band theory, magnetic properties
- **Superconductivity & Superfluidity** — low-temperature phenomena and quantum condensates
- **Materials Characterization** — X-ray diffraction, electron microscopy, spectroscopic analysis

## Current Projects

- Phase transitions in transition-metal compounds
- Electronic structure of graphene-based materials
- Magnetic ordering in frustrated lattice systems

## Facilities

- Low-temperature cryogenic systems (down to 4K)
- X-ray diffractometer
- Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM)

## Student Opportunities

UG and PG students can participate in materials synthesis, characterization, and computational modeling projects.
