## Overview

The Astrophysics & Space Physics group conducts observational and computational research on stars, galaxies, black holes, and solar phenomena, combining data from ground and space-based telescopes.

## Research Focus

- **Stellar Astrophysics** — stellar evolution, binary systems, compact objects
- **Galactic Dynamics** — structure and dynamics of the Milky Way and nearby galaxies
- **Space Weather** — solar wind, magnetospheric phenomena, geomagnetic storms

## Current Projects

- Analysis of X-ray data from Chandra and Swift satellites
- N-body simulations of galactic collisions
- Solar flare prediction models using machine learning

## Observational Access

- Collaboration with national observatories
- Archival data analysis from major space missions (Chandra, XMM-Newton, Swift)

## Educational Role

- PG students gain experience with large astronomical datasets
- Training in data analysis and computational astrophysics
