## Overview

The Theoretical & Mathematical Physics group works on fundamental problems in physics using analytical techniques, numerical simulations, and advanced mathematical frameworks, collaborating with the department's experimental groups.

## Research Focus

- **Quantum Field Theory** — renormalization, effective field theories, symmetries
- **Statistical Mechanics** — phase transitions, critical phenomena, non-equilibrium systems
- **Mathematical Methods** — group theory, differential geometry, topology in physics

## Current Projects

- Effective field theory approaches to condensed matter systems
- Stability analysis of non-equilibrium phases
- Topological aspects of quantum systems

## Computational Tools

- Numerical simulations (Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics)
- Symbolic computation software (Mathematica, SageMath)
- Scientific computing in Python and C++

## Interdisciplinary Collaborations

- Joint projects with experimental condensed matter and optics groups
- Theoretical support for observational astrophysics
