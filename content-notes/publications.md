# Publications Page

## Recommended outline

1. **Intro/summary** — brief note on department publication output, link to aggregate metrics if desired (e.g. faculty Google Scholar profiles, institutional repository)
2. **Filter/search controls** — by year, author, research area, journal
3. **Publication list** — each entry typically needs:
   - Authors (department authors highlighted/bolded)
   - Title
   - Journal/venue name, year, volume/pages
   - DOI / arXiv link (where available)
   - Optional: abstract toggle, research area tag
4. **Highlighted/featured publications** (optional) — spotlight notable papers
5. **Books/book chapters** (optional) — separate section, common on Indian university department sites
6. **Patents** (optional) — if the department has any
7. **Export/citation tools** (optional) — BibTeX download, citation format toggle
