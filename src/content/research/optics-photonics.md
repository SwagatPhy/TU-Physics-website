## Overview

The Optics & Photonics group explores light-matter interactions at quantum and classical scales, developing new techniques for quantum cryptography, optical sensing, and photonic device engineering.

## Research Focus

- **Quantum Optics** — single photon sources, quantum entanglement, quantum key distribution
- **Nonlinear Optics** — frequency conversion, harmonic generation, optical parametric processes
- **Photonic Devices** — fiber optics, integrated photonics, optical communications

## Current Projects

- Development of quantum random number generators
- Nonlinear optical crystals for UV/IR generation
- Silicon-based photonic integrated circuits

## Facilities

- Mode-locked lasers and ultrafast optics lab
- Quantum optics experimental setup
- Optical fiber characterization equipment
