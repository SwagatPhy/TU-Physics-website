## Recommended outline

1. **Intro blurb** — overview of the department's research scope/philosophy
2. **Research area/group grid** — each area as a card/section with:
   - Area/group name (e.g. Condensed Matter Physics, Nuclear & Particle Physics, Optics & Photonics, Astrophysics, Materials Science — actual list must come from the department)
   - Short description (2–4 sentences, what questions the group tackles)
   - Faculty members working in this area (linked to Faculty page)
   - Key labs/equipment used (linked to Facilities page)

3. **Funded projects** (optional) — major sponsored research projects, funding agencies (UGC, DST, SERB, CSIR, etc.)
4. **Student research opportunities** — how UG/PG/PhD students can join a group (project work, dissertations, thesis)
5. **Collaborations** (optional) — other departments/institutions the group collaborates with
