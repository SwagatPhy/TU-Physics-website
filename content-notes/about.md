# About the Department

## Recommended outline

1. **Overview** — what the department is, when established, its place within Tezpur University (School/Faculty it belongs to)
2. **History / timeline** — key milestones (department founding, program launches, notable achievements)
3. **Mission & vision statement**
4. **Organizational structure** — Head of Department, departmental committees (academic, research, etc.)
5. **Facilities & infrastructure summary** — labs, computing facilities, library/reading room (detail lives on Facilities/Labs page, this is a short pointer/summary)
6. **Affiliations** — university accreditation (NAAC, UGC recognition), collaborating institutions, MOUs
7. **Location** — where on campus, with map
8. **Leadership** — photo + short bio of Head of Department
9. **Departmental reports/newsletters** — links to downloadable reports if applicable
