# Facilities / Labs Page

## Recommended outline

1. **Intro blurb** — overview of the department's teaching and research infrastructure
2. **Teaching labs** — UG/PG laboratory courses (e.g. general physics lab, electronics lab, advanced physics lab), with brief description of what's covered
3. **Research labs** — one entry per research lab/group, each with:
   - Lab name
   - Associated research area/group (link to Research page)
   - Faculty in charge
   - Major equipment/instruments
   - Brief description of work done there
4. **Central/shared facilities** — departmental library or reading room, computing lab, workshop, any major shared instrumentation
5. **Safety & access info** (optional) — usage policy for students/external researchers
6. **Equipment list** (optional, if department wants an itemized inventory page)
