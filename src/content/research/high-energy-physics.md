## Overview

The High Energy Physics group conducts theoretical research on fundamental interactions, nuclear structure, and particle phenomenology, with strong computational and analytical components.

## Research Focus

- **Standard Model Physics** — electroweak interactions, QCD, gauge theories
- **Nuclear Structure** — nuclear reactions, alpha decay, collective excitations
- **Beyond Standard Model** — dark matter candidates, supersymmetry, extra dimensions

## Current Projects

- Phenomenological studies of LHC data
- Monte Carlo simulations for collider physics
- Nuclear shell model calculations for exotic nuclei

## Computing Resources

- High-performance computing cluster for lattice QCD
- Computational physics software suite

## International Connections

- CERN Collaborations (via theory support)
- International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) partnerships
