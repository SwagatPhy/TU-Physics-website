# Academics Page (UG / PG / PhD Programs, Courses, Admissions)

## Recommended outline

1. **Program overview** — what degrees the department offers: B.Sc. (if part of an integrated/honours program), M.Sc. Physics, Ph.D. in Physics, and any certificate/diploma courses
2. **Per-program sections**, each with:
   - Program name & degree awarded
   - Duration
   - Eligibility criteria
   - Admission process & timeline (entrance exam, merit list, counseling dates — Tezpur University typically runs its own entrance tests for PG/PhD)
   - Curriculum/semester structure outline
   - Seats available (intake)
   - Fee structure (or link to official fee page)
   - Scholarships/fellowships available (e.g. UGC/CSIR/DST INSPIRE, institute fellowship for PhD scholars)
   - How to apply (link to official Tezpur University admissions portal)
3. **Course catalog** — list of courses with codes, titles, brief descriptions, credits, semester offered
4. **Academic calendar** — key dates (application deadlines, semester start/end, exams)
5. **Syllabus downloads** — link to official syllabus PDFs per program
6. **FAQ** — common applicant questions
7. **Contact for admissions** — dedicated email/office within the department or the central university admissions cell
