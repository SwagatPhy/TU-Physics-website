# Faculty Page

## Recommended outline

### Directory (listing) page
- Filter/search by research area, name, or designation
- Grid/list of faculty cards, each with: photo thumbnail, name, designation, primary research interest tags, link to individual profile
- Consider separate groupings/tabs: Regular Faculty, Emeritus/Visiting, Technical/Support Staff (common on Indian university department sites)

### Individual faculty profile — fields typically needed
- Full name (with title, e.g. Dr./Prof.)
- Photo (professional headshot)
- Designation/title (e.g. Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, Emeritus)
- Research interests (short tag list + longer paragraph)
- Short bio (education, career history)
- Contact info: email, phone (optional), office/room number
- Links: personal/lab website, Google Scholar / ADS / ORCID / ResearchGate profile
- Selected publications (list or link to full publication list)
- Courses taught (link to Academics)
- Current research scholars (PhD students) supervised (optional)
- Administrative roles (e.g. "Coordinator, M.Sc. Program") if applicable

## Open questions for user

- Need: complete faculty roster (full names, correct titles/designations, current status — regular/emeritus/visiting/on leave).
- Need: professional headshots for each faculty member (with usage permission).
- Need: verified research interests and bios for each person (self-written preferred, to avoid inaccuracies).
- Need: official email format (e.g. name@tezu.ac.in) and whether individual emails should be public.
- Need: preferred external profile links per person (Google Scholar, ORCID, ResearchGate, personal site).
- Need: whether the site should group faculty by research area, and what those areas are officially called in the department.
- Need: policy on including emeritus/visiting faculty, PhD scholars, and technical/support staff on this page vs. separate pages.
- Need: list of courses each faculty member currently teaches (for cross-linking with Academics).
