# Home / Landing Page

## Recommended outline

1. **Hero section**
   - Department name + tagline (short mission statement, one line)
   - Background image (department building, lab, campus, classroom)
   - Primary CTA buttons (e.g. "Explore Programs", "Meet the Faculty", "Admissions")
2. **Quick intro / mission blurb** (2–3 sentences on what the department does — teaching + research)
3. **Highlights strip** — 3–4 stat tiles (e.g. "X faculty", "Y research scholars", "Z UG/PG programs", "established in YYYY")
4. **Programs preview** — quick links to UG (B.Sc.), PG (M.Sc.), and PhD programs
5. **Research areas preview** — grid/cards linking to Research page (icon + short label per area)
6. **Latest notices/announcements** — 3 most recent items, "View all" link (university depts. typically run a "Notices" board for exam dates, results, admissions, etc.)
7. **Upcoming events / seminars** — short list with dates
8. **Call-to-action band** — link to Admissions or Contact
9. **Footer** — address, quick links, Tezpur University main site link, social/media handles
